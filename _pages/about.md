I am a **Ph.D. student** at the [Guanghua School of Management, Peking University](https://en.gsm.pku.edu.cn/).

My research focuses on **Human–AI Interaction**, **Agentic AI**, and **AI Governance**.

I am interested in how people interact with AI, how AI agents participate in human activities, and how these systems can be governed responsibly. My published work explores multilingual voice interaction, comprehension, and user experience.
